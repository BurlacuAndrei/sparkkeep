# 05: Docked Board Inspector & Keyboard Navigation Parity

**What to build:** Add the non-blocking **Board Inspector** split-pane on widescreen viewports (`>= 1200px`) and full keyboard navigation across the Execution Board:
- Single-clicking a card (or pressing `Space` on a keyboard-focused card) opens/updates the docked **Board Inspector** on the right without obscuring the board canvas, displaying status/horizon selectors, pin-to-focus (`🎯`), an interactive/editable Action Checklist, Executive Brief (`tldr`, `why_care`, `claims`), and Research Dossier preview—with a 1-click **Expand (`o`)** button (or double-click) to launch the full two-column Card Modal.
- Add board keyboard shortcuts (`j`/`k` or arrow keys to navigate cards, `Space` to toggle the Board Inspector, `o`/`Enter` for the full modal, `[` and `]` to move a card across status columns, `1`/`2`/`3` to assign Horizon, `f` to pin to Focus Spotlight, and `?` for the keyboard shortcuts help modal).

**Blocked by:** 01: Widescreen Fluid Execution Board, Execution Pulse & Facet Filtering, 02: Stage-Aware Cards, Inline Action Checklist & Final-Step Completion Payoff, 03: Focus Spotlight ("Next Easiest Step" Recommendation Banner)

**Status:** complete

- [x] Clicking a card on the Execution Board on widescreen viewports (`>= 1200px`, or pressing `Space` on a focused card) opens or updates the non-blocking **Board Inspector** pane docked alongside the board canvas, while double-clicking a card (or clicking on narrower viewports `< 1200px`) invokes `onSelectCard` to open the full Card Modal.
- [x] The **Board Inspector** displays the card's title, platform/type badges, quick Status and Horizon selectors, **Pin to Focus (`🎯`)** toggle, interactive Action Checklist (supporting checking/unchecking, adding, and removing steps), Executive Brief (`tldr`, `why_care`, `claims`, `open_questions`), and Research Verdict / Dossier preview (or a **Deep Research** trigger button).
- [x] Clicking the **Expand (`Maximize2`)** button in the Board Inspector (or pressing `o` / `Enter`) closes or promotes the inspector selection into the full Card Modal via `onSelectCard`, and clicking **Close (`X` / `Escape`)** closes the docked inspector.
- [x] Keyboard navigation on the Execution Board supports: `j`/`ArrowDown` and `k`/`ArrowUp` to move card focus across visible board cards; `Space` to toggle the Board Inspector; `o`/`Enter` to open the full Card Modal; `[` and `]` to move the focused card left/right across execution stages (`to-do` ↔ `in-progress` ↔ `done`); `1`/`2`/`3` to set Horizon (`short-term`, `medium-term`, `long-term`); `f` to toggle pinning the focused card in the Focus Spotlight; and `?` to open a Board Keyboard Shortcuts modal.
- [x] All Board Inspector and keyboard shortcut strings are localized across all 6 Interface Locales (`en`, `ro`, `es`, `pt-BR`, `fr`, `de`), and automated tests verify docked inspector opening/switching/expanding, checklist editing inside the inspector, and keyboard shortcut actions.
