# 0003. Playbook Studio Workspace, Timeline Sequencer & Non-Destructive Taxonomy Cascade

Research playbooks were previously buried inside the Settings modal with a cramped form list, fragmented step authoring, and ambiguous behavior when deleting user-defined card types. We decided to promote Playbooks to a first-class, full-width workspace view (`Playbook Studio`) featuring an interactive visual sequential Pipeline Timeline with in-line Step Primitive insertion and client-side prompt dry-run inspection, while implementing a Non-Destructive Taxonomy Cascade that safely detaches deleted card types without destroying user-created playbooks. This elevates research authoring to a flagship, distraction-free creative experience while guaranteeing domain asset safety across configurable taxonomy changes.
