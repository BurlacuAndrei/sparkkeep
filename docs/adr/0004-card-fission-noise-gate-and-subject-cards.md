# 0004. Card Fission Policy, Triage Noise Gate, and Subject Cards

Captured content previously over-fragmented into multiple cards for single posts, allowed zero-signal engagement bait into the Inbox, and blocked AI triage and deep research for user-authored cards without URLs. We decided to: (1) adopt a consolidated single-card default for posts and threads with a hard ceiling of at most 3 cards per capture, triggering card fission only when genuinely distinct standalone subjects, video chapters, or tools are identified; (2) introduce a Triage Noise Gate that detects engagement bait and pure junk promotion, discarding them entirely so no cards are generated; and (3) treat user-authored cards as first-class Subject Cards with on-demand triage synthesis and autonomous research that ground directly on the user's initial topic notes. This prevents inbox bloat from social media hype while enabling open-ended subject investigation.
